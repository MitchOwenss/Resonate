      Tips for accurate tracking

      • Use headphones so the guide tones don't leak into the mic (listening pauses during tones anyway).
      • Practise somewhere quiet, about 30 cm from the mic.
      • Open vowels ("ah", "oo") and humming track best.
      • Stop if you feel pain, tickling or hoarseness. Sip water between exercises.
