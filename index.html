For accurate tracking

  • Use headphones so guide tones don't leak into the mic.
  • Practise somewhere quiet, about 30 cm from the mic.
  • Open vowels ("ah", "oo") and humming track best.
  • Stop if you feel pain or hoarseness, and sip water between exercises.
